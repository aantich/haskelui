# Bundled TextMate resource provenance

The initial Haskell, JSON, JavaScript, Python, and Markdown grammar fixtures and the
Visual Haskell Light and Dark themes in this directory were authored for Visual Haskell
in 2026 and are distributed under the repository's BSD-3-Clause license.

They intentionally exercise the supported TextMate compatibility surface
without importing third-party grammar licensing obligations. Users can add
full VS Code/TextMate extension directories under `~/.vh/extensions`.

The regex engine is Oniguruma 6.9.10. Its upstream license is retained under
`vendor/oniguruma/COPYING`.
